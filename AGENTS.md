# ReactantNitro.jl conventions

## Prose

**No em dashes or en dashes anywhere**, in code, comments, docstrings, documents, or commit
messages. Use a comma, a colon, a semicolon, or restructure the sentence.

**Use simple and precise language**, do not orbit to the point, just make it in the most simple way possible. Strive for
the minimum possible representation that clearly makes the point.

**It's absolutely unacceptable to have paragraph long comment blocks added for every single change or discovery.**
